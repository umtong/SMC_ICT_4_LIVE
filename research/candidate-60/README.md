## Preserved evidence

- The immediate four-hour jump-reversal family is unstable, but waiting at least two completed five-minute bars for a reversal close through the terminal jump-candle extreme survived its latest untouched short test. It is retained as a sparse high-payoff post-cascade specialist.

## Integration rule

Candidate 60 will not add a weak strategy to manufacture trade count. A new scenario family enters the final system only when it solves a distinct market-state problem and remains useful in the actual one-slot continuous account. The final target remains one decision policy, one capital path and one realistic cost-after NAV—not a sum of separate backtests.
