# UOAM predeclared research ledger

## Prior failure incorporated

HML's full-response variant passed the first frozen BTC week but failed both
unchanged holdouts.  AFHR showed that stale-context suppression reduced loss
clustering but did not create positive expectancy.  ADOM proved that passively
buying or selling the defense origin increased coverage while destroying hit
rate.  The next candidate therefore does not add another timing threshold or
entry-price optimization.

## Market mechanism

A completed directional auction is tradable only while a liquidity objective
that existed before that impulse remains unresolved.  The objective cannot be a
swing or equal high/low discovered after the impulse, and it cannot have been
traded through by the accepting bar.  The target is bound to the context before
any lower-timeframe entry forms.  Once consumed, it cannot be reused; the next
pre-existing objective becomes active.  Full rebalance of the accepting impulse
origin, accepted-boundary loss, or bias replacement invalidates the context.

This implements objective formation, binding, consumption and invalidation as
states rather than choosing the nearest attractive target at entry time.

## Fixed parent contract

The following are unchanged from the committed HML full-response experiment:

- 60-minute completed accepted auction;
- 5-minute right-confirmed swing and equal-high/low pools;
- bias and response signed-flow evidence; sweep flow is not required;
- full-sweep-bar response break;
- stop construction, minimum structural RR and 60-minute holding timeout;
- one-bar causal entry delay, favorable-drift guard and cost gate;
- whole-account NAV risk sizing at three percent planned loss;
- NautilusTrader fills, fees, positions and NAV;
- frozen BTC weeks and all promotion gates.

## Controlled variants

1. **Full:** bound objective lifecycle plus scenario-declared pending-entry and
   open-position invalidation.
2. **One-variable ablation:** identical objective lifecycle, but an already-open
   position is left to the unchanged bracket and timeout.
3. **Reference:** original dynamic-nearest HML target contract; ineligible and
   required to reproduce committed HML metrics.

Selection is the first eligible gate-qualified variant in this fixed order.
There is no result-driven parameter rescue.  If neither eligible variant passes
the first week, UOAM is discarded.  If one passes, its configuration is locked
before the two sealed weeks are opened.
