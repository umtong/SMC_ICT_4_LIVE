# Liquidity Auction V6 structural research

V6 continues from the strict-causal V5 work without discarding the economically useful first-return hypothesis.

The research target is one coherent skilled-trader policy:

1. represent market structure with pre-existing horizontal semantic liquidity and causally confirmed diagonal/channel or volatility-normalized event-time boundaries;
2. at the interaction, sequentially distinguish genuine acceptance, failed auction/absorption, and unresolved balance using price progress relative to aggressive flow, outside dwell/value migration, common-market breadth and derivative ownership where available;
3. choose WAIT, ARM FIRST-RETURN LIMIT, or REJECT rather than placing every return order immediately or chasing a completed response at market;
4. refine the first-return price with an already-known boundary, OB, FVG, BPR or IFVG;
5. predeclare a causal invalidation stop and an already-existing opposing liquidity target paying at least 1R gross;
6. cancel only unfilled orders whose original opportunity is invalidated, spent, passed, or replaced by a clearly stronger independent opportunity;
7. after fill, exit only at the predeclared take-profit or stop-loss;
8. arbitrate BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT and XRPUSDT through one global pending-order/position slot.

This branch contains development implementations for causal fourth-point channel rejection, directional-change external failed auction and hierarchical continuation, sequential acceptance-versus-rejection inference, semantic/diagonal/derivative confluence, account-level pending-order replacement, and trade/no-trade chart clinics. Results that do not survive separated periods and a fresh window are not rescued by looser thresholds.
