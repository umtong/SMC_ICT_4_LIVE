# Candidate 11 cumulative research decision ledger

This ledger is append-only in meaning: later research may supersede a decision, but it must state the new evidence and may not silently recycle a rejected assumption.

## Iteration 1 — dense pivot sweep model

**Hypothesis:** every causally confirmed local pivot represents actionable external liquidity.

**Observed defect:** the map produced far more sweep events than coherent session auctions. Nearest-pivot targets shortened structural room and transformed micro-noise into SMC labels.

**Decision:** rejected as a logic failure. Local pivots remain internal MSS/inducement evidence; they are not default external targets.

## Iteration 2 — dual auction FAR/AAC

**Hypothesis:** a liquidity trade-through starts competing failed-auction and accepted-auction explanations.

**Implementation lessons:**

- visual pivots are unavailable until right-side confirmation;
- FAR and AAC cannot share raid/impulse extremes;
- AAC must freeze the pre-pullback impulse extreme;
- accepted-boundary stops belong behind the accepted boundary;
- a one-minute bar that sweeps both sides has unknowable path order and is rejected.

**Decision:** retained as the scenario skeleton.

## Iteration 3 — session-framed SCDAM

**Hypothesis:** completed session ranges are more stable and transferable auction references than dense local pivots.

**Changes:** New-York/DST-aware source/target sessions, final raid episode rather than first cross, causal post-raid MSS for FAR, persistent outside acceptance and causal pullback/reacceleration for AAC, preconfirmed external targets only.

**Status:** retained pending actual Nautilus execution. Signal-only target-first observations do not count as wins or NAV evidence.

## Iteration 4 — reproducible execution repair

**Implementation failure:** the pinned pandas/Nautilus combination exposed a read-only array to `BarDataWrangler` before strategy execution.

**Repair:** build official Nautilus `Bar` objects directly at completed-observation timestamps. Nautilus remains solely responsible for event order, orders, fills, fees, margin, positions and NAV.

**Research control:** implementation failures are repaired on the unchanged frozen W1 interval. A normally completed but weak W1 is classified as a logic/expectancy failure rather than routed to another period.

## Iteration 5 — four-market global portfolio

**Hypothesis:** BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP should provide independent auction opportunities without per-symbol parameter fitting.

**Controls:** identical detector semantics, deterministic same-timestamp arbitration, and one global pending-entry/open-position mutex. Ranking does not alter the exact 3% NAV risk rate.

**Status:** portfolio infrastructure implemented. Activation requires BTC W1 to show positive after-cost expectancy with insufficient opportunity count, or an explicit four-market W1 experiment using the unchanged logic.

## Iteration 6 — cross-market confirmation

**Hypothesis:** correlated market confirmation separates broad price discovery from an idiosyncratic stop run.

- FAR evidence strengthens when one market raids its normalized source boundary while the leave-one-out peer median does not confirm.
- AAC evidence strengthens when at least three markets close beyond homologous source boundaries.

**Controls:** same completed timestamp only, dimensionless source-range locations, no fixed BTC benchmark, no future bars, no score-based risk multiplier.

**Status:** unit-tested causal evidence layer and frozen-W1 structural diagnostic added. It must improve independent plan quality/frequency before being wired into live approval.

## Iteration 7 — online selective approval

**Hypothesis:** regime durability requires abstention when the lower confidence bound of realized after-cost log growth is not positive.

**Controls:** only terminal chronological outcomes update calibration; exact bucket then scenario-level hierarchical fallback; recent error-rate change pauses new entries; approved trades retain 3% NAV risk.

**Status:** unit-tested approval layer. It is not used to manufacture historical performance and must be trained only on data preceding each decision.

## Advancement rule

1. W1 must produce complete Nautilus run/data/order/position/account/event/plan/metric evidence.
2. The independent audit must recompute daily geometric NAV growth and verify exact 3% risk, no overlap, no liquidation and no engine errors.
3. Implementation failure: repair and rerun W1.
4. No plans or weak after-cost expectancy: logic failure; perform one controlled causal ablation or replace the hypothesis.
5. Positive expectancy but insufficient frequency: unchanged four-market extension on W1.
6. Only a passed W1 screening gate unlocks untouched W2, then W3, then long evaluation.
7. No single screening week may authorize a durable project-success claim.
