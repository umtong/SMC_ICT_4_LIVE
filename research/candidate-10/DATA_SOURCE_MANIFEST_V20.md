## Timestamp audit contract

- Klines become observable only at their close time.
- Aggregate trades use their transaction timestamp.
- Metrics use the raw `create_time` field; promotion requires documenting whether that field is a snapshot/publication time or an interval boundary.
- Same-timestamp metrics are not assumed safe merely because their numeric timestamp is less than or equal to a bar close. A conservative one-interval-lag sensitivity is required before promotion when publication semantics remain undocumented.
