# Why short random weeks repeatedly look good and continuous evaluation fails

## Decision

A positive short week is no longer treated as validation. It is only evidence
that a frozen candidate was not falsified in that sample. A negative week may
still terminate a weak family early, but a positive week cannot establish
positive long-run expectancy.

This change is necessary because Candidate 05 has accumulated many candidate
families and ablations over the same small set of weeks. After the first result
was observed those weeks became development data, regardless of whether each
later change was described as a logical rather than numerical change.

## Measured implementation confound

The single-symbol BTC runner installed the causal premium-index/basis feature
contract before importing the backtest module. The shared-account runner did
not. Since the shared backtest imports `load_range` at module import time, the
shared route silently omitted premium-index change fields used by the
positioning-reset gate. A missing value is conservatively false, so early BTC
entries which passed in the single runner were deferred or removed in the
shared runner.

This was an evaluation-path implementation error, not evidence that the market
logic transferred poorly. The shared runner now installs the identical basis
contract before importing the base runner, patches the base module to the same
loader, and has a contract test proving the installed loader identity. Results
before that repair may not be used to compare single-symbol and shared-account
market logic.

## Why positive weeks still fail after execution parity

### 1. Candidate-family selection turns a random week into a selected right tail

A week with seven trades and six wins appears persuasive, but it is not strong
population evidence. Under a 50% Bernoulli null, six or more wins in seven occur
with probability 6.25%. Across fifty distinct candidate variants, the chance
that at least one produces such an outcome is approximately 96%. The project
has explored enough versions that this multiple-comparison effect cannot be
ignored.

The exact trades are not independent Bernoulli observations, which makes the
nominal sample size even smaller. Several trades can arise from one persistent
liquidation or repricing episode. Seven positions can therefore represent two
or three effective market episodes.

### 2. The same visible pattern mixes incompatible economic causes

A sweep, reclaim, CHoCH and retest can be generated by:

- temporary liquidation and dealer inventory exhaustion;
- durable common-market price discovery;
- a small counter-rotation inside an accepted higher-timeframe auction;
- a local move while peer assets continue the original direction;
- news or volatility expansion whose first reclaim is only transient.

A favorable week can be dominated by the first cause. A continuous sample
contains all five. Candidate 05 evidence repeatedly showed that tightening a
causal chain collapses opportunity density, while loosening it admits the wrong
auction and creates negative expectancy. This is the core structural frontier,
not a target or leverage problem.

### 3. A new branch has often inherited old winners

When a new family submits zero orders, total strategy metrics can remain good
because inherited paths traded. That is not incremental alpha. Promotion now
requires explicit branch attribution: a new family must contribute actual
Nautilus positions and positive cost-after incremental expectancy. Inherited
orders may be a control but may not be credited to the new hypothesis.

### 4. Restarted weeks are not the same experiment as slices of one continuous run

Every standalone week starts with:

- a fresh account and full NAV;
- a fresh strategy process;
- a short warm-up;
- empty active-pool, parent-auction, peer-context and entry-slot state;
- no unresolved scenario carried from the preceding market period.

The same calendar dates inside a continuous run can begin with live pools,
accepted context, parent-event locks, reduced NAV, or an occupied global slot.
Weekly boundaries can also truncate losses, pending scenarios and recovery
paths. Therefore independently restarted weekly runs can follow a different
strategy state path even when the source code and market bars are identical.

The repository now contains an exact controlled audit which runs v46 once over
2024-03-01 through 2024-03-30 and also as five independently restarted blocks
covering the same dates. It compares each restarted block with the identical
dates inside the continuous Nautilus NAV path. Any difference is boundary or
state-reset path dependence, not market sampling.

### 5. Losses cluster while the 3% budget compounds multiplicatively

At a 3% planned loss, four full losses compound NAV to `0.97^4 = 0.8853`, a
loss of 11.47%; recovery then requires 12.96%. Ten full losses leave 73.74% of
NAV and require 35.61% to recover. This is not an argument to lower the required
3% risk. It means a scenario must avoid correlated wrong-regime losses; average
single-trade expectancy is insufficient.

Many Candidate 05 targets are valid from 0.40 to 1.50 post-cost R. At 0.40R the
break-even win rate is 71.43%; at 0.75R it is 57.14%. A short favorable run can
clear those rates, while a modest long-run drop in directional accuracy produces
negative geometric growth and clustered drawdown.

### 6. The one-slot shared account changes which opportunity is realized

Single BTC evidence does not automatically transfer to the project account.
ETH or SOL may acquire the only executable slot before a later BTC setup. The
weak shared week previously showed profitable BTC attribution alongside large
ETH/SOL losses. This does not justify symbol-specific thresholds; it shows that
the causal state was not sufficiently market-general and that opportunity
competition must be evaluated in the one-account path from the beginning.

### 7. Feature meaning changes across regimes even when thresholds are fixed

Percentage-band book depth, normalized aggressive flow, open-interest change
and premium-index normalization do not have constant information content. A
fixed visible imbalance can mean genuine withdrawal in one volatility regime
and ordinary inventory relocation in another. Long samples expose this
semantic drift; a week can accidentally contain a regime where the fixed
contract is well calibrated.

## Corrected research protocol

1. **A short block is a falsification screen only.** Failure can discard a
   family. Success only permits further testing.
2. **Freeze the candidate commit before untouched evidence.** No rule, target,
   branch or threshold may change after the holdout draw.
3. **A viewed week becomes development data permanently.** Reusing it is allowed
   only for controlled implementation diagnosis, never as OOS evidence.
4. **Use continuous stateful replay for positive evidence.** Report rolling
   seven-day slices from one continuous Nautilus run rather than resetting the
   strategy, account and context for every week.
5. **Compare restarted and embedded paths explicitly.** Any difference must be
   explained before performance interpretation.
6. **Require incremental branch attribution.** A branch with zero positions or
   non-positive incremental cost-after expectancy cannot advance on inherited
   strategy metrics.
7. **Measure independent auction episodes as well as raw trades.** The episode
   measure is diagnostic and does not add a risk cap; it prevents five trades
   from one repricing chain being mistaken for five independent confirmations.
8. **Promote on cumulative multi-block evidence, not a per-week jackpot.** A
   frozen candidate must show positive continuous NAV across multiple untouched
   blocks and then survive the shared four-symbol account.
9. **Keep the project risk and execution contracts unchanged.** Current-NAV 3%
   loss sizing, costs, adverse slippage, Nautilus matching, liquidation and the
   single global slot remain authoritative.

## Research implication

The recurring week-to-long failure is not repaired by adding another score or
by selecting the next attractive week. The loop ends only when a frozen causal
state produces positive incremental expectancy in continuous replay, survives
multiple untouched blocks without restart assistance, and retains that
expectancy when BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP compete for one shared account slot.
