## Negative cases required for future expansion

The next source-elicitation pass should keep examples where:

- a visible OB/FVG was ignored;
- a target zone was never reached and the order was canceled;
- a break became acceptance rather than fakeout;
- a similar-looking fakeout failed;
- a channel fourth point rotated, broke and held, or re-entered;
- the trader declined a setup solely because confirmation consumed the target space;
- BTC/peer behavior changed an alt decision;
- a locally attractive long was rejected because broad selling persisted, or a locally attractive short was rejected because the market leader and peers remained strong;
- the highest-volume or strongest-trend instrument was *not* selected, so the boundary of the instrument-selection cue can be reconstructed.

A new rule is not accepted merely because it explains winners. It must also distinguish these negative cases before the outcome is known.
