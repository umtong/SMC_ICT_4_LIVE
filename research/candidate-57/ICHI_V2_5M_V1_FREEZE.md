# Candidate 57 — public ichiV2 five-minute family v1 freeze

## Why this external family is being tested

A public Freqtrade result labeled `ichiV2` reports 1,056 completed spot trades in roughly 92 days (11.48/day), 76.6% wins, profit factor 6.51, approximately 68 minutes average holding time, 3,532.95% account return, and 1.13% account drawdown. The report includes XRP, SOL and ETH results, directly overlapping three assets in the project universe.

Those figures are not project evidence. They used a broad spot universe, up to three simultaneous trades, and an unreported configuration. They are a high-information discovery signal that justifies a source-faithful project test.

## External source components preserved

The entry state is the public `ichiV2.py` logic:

1. completed five-minute candles only;
2. qtpylib recursive Heikin-Ashi open/high/low;
3. raw close and Heikin-Ashi open shifted by one completed candle;
4. Ichimoku computed from the one-candle-shifted transformed OHLC with periods 20/60/120 and displacement 30;
5. chikou span never used;
6. five-minute trend close above both displaced cloud spans;
7. five-minute, fifteen-minute, thirty-minute and one-hour trend closes above their corresponding Heikin-Ashi-open EMA tracks;
8. one-hour / eight-hour fan magnitude above one;
9. fan magnitude gain at least 1.0013 and fan magnitude increasing over the prior three completed candles;
10. source exit when the five-minute trend close crosses below the 1.5-hour trend close.

The `ichiV2_5` source variant changes only the public parameters it declares: fan gain 1.0007, two increasing fan candles, 6% stop, 3% trailing distance after a 40% offset, and no ROI table.

## Explicit project adaptations

- The original strategy is long-only spot. Long-only variants preserve that. Mirrored short and both-side variants are declared adaptations and are never described as source-exact.
- The project universe is BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT and XRPUSDT.
- Cross-symbol collisions are resolved by a causal score using cloud distance, fan magnitude distance and fan gain distance. Candidate rows are not orders; one winner is submitted.
- One global pending entry or open position is enforced.
- Current-NAV 3% planned-loss sizing, project fees, adverse slippage and funding reserve apply.
- Maximum holding time is 480 minutes, preserving day-trading.

## Management ambiguity and predeclared profiles

The public result cannot have been generated by the visible `ichiV2.py` defaults: the code declares 30% ROI and a 10% stop, while the report shows ROI exits averaging 1.52% and a worst trade of -3.88%. Therefore two interpretations are separated rather than silently reconciled.
