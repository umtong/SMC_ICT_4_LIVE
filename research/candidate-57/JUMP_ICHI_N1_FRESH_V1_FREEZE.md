# Candidate 57 — Jump + Ichi N→1 fresh v1 freeze

This file freezes the policy before the scored interval is downloaded or
replayed. The experiment asks whether two already-supported but incomplete
families solve different account problems when routed through one real slot:

- **4h jump reversal specialist** — a completed 4h log return at least two
  causal prior-only standard deviations from its prior 18-return distribution,
  traded opposite the impulse; whole-impulse extreme plus one terminal ATR
  invalidation; least absolute already-qualified z-score selects among
  simultaneous symbols; immediate entry; 240-minute source horizon; transient
  +0.4R after-cost break-even protection that disarms after +1.0R escape.
- **public ichiV2 report-short-level continuation** — completed five-minute
  multi-horizon fan/cloud state, shifted source inputs, 4% stop, 8% emergency
  objective, 1.5% ROI ignored while the entry state remains active, public
  1.5h source crossover exit, and 480-minute account horizon.

## One-account arbitration

Three cells use identical data, costs, latency, fills, current-NAV 3% planned
loss sizing and NautilusTrader accounting:

1. `jump_only`
2. `ichi_only`
3. `integrated`

At a completed 4h boundary, an actionable jump event has priority over an
ichi signal because it represents a narrower abnormal-return state. On all
other completed five-minute boundaries, ichi may act. An open position or
pending entry is never preempted. Signals observed while the slot is busy are
recorded as shadow diagnostics but cannot trade. There is no handoff, taker
filter, OI filter, symbol exception, outcome score, threshold search or
post-result parameter change.

## Data freeze

The scored entry window is **2024-02-01 through 2024-02-29 UTC**. It was chosen
by the predeclared deterministic rule “earliest complete calendar month from
2024 onward absent from the branch-wide date/evidence search,” not by viewing
its market outcome. Data from 2024-01-12 through 2024-01-31 are entry-disabled
startup; 2024-03-01 through 2024-03-02 are entry-disabled close runoff.

## Causal predictions before replay

- The integrated account should contain jump and ichi episodes; differences
  from standalone cells should be explainable by the single-slot path and
  explicit jump priority.
- The jump family’s positive winner engine, including its best winner, should
  survive integration rather than being displaced by generic continuation.
- Ichi should add independent non-jump opportunities and raise completed trade
  density.
- A valid composition should improve both signal-window geometric growth and
  completed trade count versus the stronger standalone account, not merely
  produce a positive total from one new outlier.
- If frequency rises while expectancy, profit factor or geometric growth falls,
  or if jump winners are displaced, the composition is rejected without
  tuning either source.

## Strict short-window target

The integrated account meets the project’s short-window target only if all
mechanics are valid and it has at least 29 completed independent trades,
after-cost signal-window geometric daily growth of at least 1%, positive
expectancy, profit factor above 1, maximum drawdown no greater than 20%, and
positive equity throughout. Even a pass authorizes stronger untouched and
continuous validation, not production deployment.
