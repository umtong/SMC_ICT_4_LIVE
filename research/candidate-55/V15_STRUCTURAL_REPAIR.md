# V15 structural repair — research decision

The raw V15 short result was not discarded because its fresh 30-day NAV was
negative.  It generated substantial gross profit and sufficient opportunity
density, while most damage came from full planned-loss exits.  The source
"short" label also concealed two different causal mechanisms.

The repair therefore preserves the source opportunity engine and management,
but routes each mechanism into the market state where it made economic sense:

1. **DI pullback resumption** — a non-negative local 15-minute pullback inside a
   negative four-hour auction, followed by the original V15 DI short edge.
2. **BB clean synchronized expansion** — the original lower-band edge only
   when the 15-minute downside displacement is at least 1.5 current five-minute
   ATR and at least three of BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP are negative over 60 minutes.

These are broad sign/plateau relationships observed across three development
intervals, not per-symbol exceptions or a search for the best single threshold.

The original source entry, 10x-normalized stop, source trailing, realistic
costs, exact 3% current-NAV planned-loss sizing, NautilusTrader execution and
one global slot remain unchanged.

Untouched validation proceeds from two short windows to a 30-day continuous
account.  A 90-day replay is allowed only when the short and medium results
already contain a strong after-cost integrated profit engine.  Every result is
decomposed into gross profit, gross loss, full-stop concentration, scenario
family and symbol rather than being reduced to a positive/negative gate.
